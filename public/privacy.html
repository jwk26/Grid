Privacy

Grid · last updated 1 November 2026

Grid is a personal task management app on a 2D grid, built and run by one person as a portfolio project. This page says what it collects, why, where it is kept and how to delete it.

What Grid collects

  • Your tasks. The Nodes, Bits, Chunks and notes you create. They belong to your account and are used only to show them back to you.
  • Guest accounts. When you open Grid without signing in, it creates an anonymous account. It holds no name or email address, only your tasks.
  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with GitHub or Google, Grid receives your name, email address and profile picture. They are used only to identify your account.
  • Bot check. A Cloudflare Turnstile check protects guest sign-in. Cloudflare processes the check under its own privacy policy.

What Grid does not do

Grid has no advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts, and does not sell or share your data. Your tasks are not read by anyone other than you, except as needed to keep the service running or fix a fault.

Where it is kept

Your account and tasks are stored in a Supabase Postgres database in Seoul, South Korea. The app is served by Vercel, which keeps ordinary request logs. Your browser stores only device settings (such as theme and sort order) and the sign-in session on your device.

How long it is kept

A guest account that has not been opened for 7 days is deleted with all its tasks. A signed-in account is kept until you delete it.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open the account menu and choose Delete account. Your account is signed out at once and cannot be used again; a daily cleanup then deletes the account and every task in it, within 24 hours. This cannot be undone.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/jwk26/Grid/issues. Issues are public, so do not put your email address or other personal details in one; say only that you have a request and it will be answered there.
